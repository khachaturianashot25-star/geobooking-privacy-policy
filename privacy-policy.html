GeoBooking AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

GeoBooking AI is a booking automation application designed to help small businesses manage customer reservation requests through supported messaging platforms.

1. Information We Collect

When a customer interacts with GeoBooking AI through a supported messaging platform, the application may process information necessary to handle the customer's booking request.

This information may include:

  • Customer name or username provided through the messaging platform
  • Messages sent to the business through the supported messaging platform
  • Reservation date and time
  • Number of guests
  • Other information voluntarily provided by the customer for the purpose of making a reservation

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by GeoBooking AI is used to:

  • Receive and process reservation requests
  • Check reservation availability
  • Create and manage reservations
  • Respond to customer booking messages
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of the application

3. Messaging Platform Data

GeoBooking AI may use APIs and webhooks provided by Meta and its supported messaging platforms to receive and respond to customer messages.

Information received through these services is processed only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and for providing the requested booking functionality.

4. Data Storage

Reservation information may be stored in the application's database for the purpose of managing reservations and preventing scheduling conflicts.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the application.

5. Data Sharing

GeoBooking AI does not sell customer personal information.

Information may be processed through third-party services when necessary to provide the application's functionality, including messaging platform APIs and hosting or infrastructure providers.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by the application. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Reservation information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide reservation management functionality and maintain the application's operation.

8. User Requests

If you have questions about information processed by GeoBooking AI, or would like to request information about your data, you may contact the application owner using the contact information provided with the application.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or GeoBooking AI, please contact the application owner through the contact method associated with the project.
